How to Study (Performance + Well-being)

Practical guardrails
  • Define “done”: one concrete output per session (e.g., 10 problems, 2 pages summarized).
  • Start hard, then easy: do the most demanding work when attention is highest.
  • Use breaks actively: stand up, water, light movement, sunlight if possible.
  • Protect sleep: if you’re consistently sleep-deprived, lower daily load rather than grinding.
  • If overwhelmed: reduce session length (e.g., 45/15) and focus on minimum viable progress.
This tool supports structure and reflection, but it’s not medical care. If anxiety, depression, or burnout feels persistent or severe, consider reaching out to a trusted professional or your school’s support services.
